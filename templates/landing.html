What's been happening?

Describe the situation in plain language. The tool will identify the risk level, flag any safeguarding concerns, and give you a clear set of next steps.

What to include

  • What type of behaviour — noise, threats, harassment, criminal damage
  • How often it happens and how long it has been going on
  • Who is affected and how — lost sleep, fear, children at home
  • Whether it has escalated or changed recently
  • Any action already taken — reports, warnings, police involvement

Before you submit: remove or replace any names, addresses, and other details that identify individuals. Focus on what happened, not who did it.

How the assessment works

The tool reads your case description and identifies factors that the evidence shows matter in ASB risk: the type of behaviour, how often it is happening, how long it has been going on, whether anyone involved is vulnerable, signs of escalation, and whether there are elements of hatred or violence.

Each factor is weighted according to a structured risk framework built from frontline practice. The risk level — low, medium, or high — is produced by that framework, not by a black-box model. On your results page you can see exactly which factors were identified and how the risk level was reached.

The more specific your description, the more accurate the result. If the assessment looks wrong, go back and add more detail.

Who's behind this

ASB Guide is built by an ASB officer with nineteen years of frontline experience in social housing in Wales. That means managing cases, supervising housing teams, and developing the risk tools and practice approaches used across the sector.

This is not a general-purpose AI product. It is a practitioner tool — built to reflect what actually works in casework, not what looks good on a product page.
