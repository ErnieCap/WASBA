Is your neighbour situation anti-social behaviour?

Describe what's been happening and get a structured risk assessment — the same framework housing teams use. Free, takes two minutes, no name or address needed.

How it works

1
Describe what's happening

Write what you've experienced — noise, intimidation, damage, harassment. Plain language is fine.

2
Get your risk assessment

The tool identifies the risk level, flags safeguarding concerns, and explains how the assessment was reached.

3
Know what to do next

Clear, prioritised next steps — who to contact, what to record, and how to make a complaint that gets taken seriously.

How it's helped people

Real situations, anonymised.

Social housing tenant, North England

A year of intimidating behaviour dismissed as a "neighbour dispute." After using ASB Guide to document the pattern, their landlord issued a formal warning within six weeks.

Private renter, Midlands

Didn't know whether their situation counted as ASB. The free assessment identified it as medium-risk and gave them a clear framework for their complaint. Their council opened an investigation.

Family, shared ownership property

Prolonged noise affecting children's sleep — months of informal reports, nothing acted on. The assessment and written report gave their housing officer something concrete to work with.

Built by a practitioner

ASB Guide was created by a housing professional with 19 years of frontline ASB and case management experience. The approach here reflects what actually works in practice — not what looks good on paper.

Your privacy

No name, no address, no account required. Keep notes factual — focus on what happened, when, and how it affected you.
