How it works

Tell us what's been happening, in your own words. We'll assess the risk level, pull out the key factors, and give you a clear set of next steps — the same framework housing teams use to triage and prioritise cases. It's free, takes about two minutes, and doesn't ask for your name or address.

How ASB Guide has helped people

Real situations, anonymised. People who came here not knowing what to do — and left with a plan.

Social housing tenant, North England

A year of intimidating behaviour dismissed as "neighbour dispute." After using ASB Guide to document the pattern, their landlord issued a formal warning within six weeks.

Private renter, Midlands

Didn't know whether their situation counted as ASB. The free assessment identified it as medium-risk. They used the written report to make a formal complaint and their council opened an investigation.

Family, shared ownership property

Prolonged noise affecting children's sleep — months of informal reports, nothing acted on. The written assessment gave their housing officer something concrete to work with.

Why ASB Guide?

ASB Guide was created by a housing professional who has spent 19 years working directly on ASB cases, leading housing teams, and developing approaches now adopted across the sector. The tools here reflect what actually works in practice — not what looks good on paper.

Your privacy

ASB Guide does not ask for your name or address. There are no accounts. Keep notes factual — focus on what happened, when, and how it affected you.
