Community Trigger details
What is the Community Trigger?
The Anti-Social Behaviour Case Review (Community Trigger) is a statutory right under the Anti-Social Behaviour, Crime and Policing Act 2014. You can request a case review if you have made 3 or more complaints about the same issue within 6 months — although your local area may set a lower threshold. The threshold is about complaints made, not whether agencies responded. Send your request to your local council or police; they will confirm whether the threshold is met and arrange a multi-agency review involving all relevant agencies.
